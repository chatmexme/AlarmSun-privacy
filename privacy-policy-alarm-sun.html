Privacy Policy – Alarm Sun

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Application Name: Alarm Sun

Version: 1.0.0

1. Introduction

Welcome to Alarm Sun (“we,” “our,” or “the App”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Alarm Sun accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

Alarm Sun is an alarm clock application designed to help users manage alarms, schedules, and wake-up challenges, including object scanning challenges.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparency about the permissions and information required for the application to function.

By using Alarm Sun, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Alarm Sun is designed to operate primarily using information stored locally on your device.

2.1 Alarm and Schedule Information

The application may store the following information locally:

  • Alarm times and schedules.
  • Alarm names and labels.
  • Repeat days and alarm configurations.
  • Alarm sound and vibration preferences.
  • Challenge configurations.
  • Application settings and preferences.
  • Alarm activity and relevant application logs.

This information is used to provide the application’s core functionality.

2.2 Camera Information

Alarm Sun requests camera access exclusively when you configure or use an Object Scan challenge.

Camera access is used to:

  • Capture images of objects for scan challenges.
  • Verify objects as part of the alarm dismissal process.
  • Support the application’s object-based wake-up challenge.

Camera permission is not required for ordinary alarm functionality.

Camera Data: The handling, temporary processing, storage, and transmission of captured images depend on the application’s actual implementation. Camera data should not be retained or transmitted beyond the disclosed functionality.

2.3 Personal Information

Alarm Sun does not require users to create an account to use its core alarm functionality.

The application does not intentionally request personal information such as:

  • Full name.
  • Residential address.
  • Phone number.
  • Government identification.
  • Personal contacts.

If future versions introduce additional features that require personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

3. Device Permissions & Exact Alarms

Alarm Sun requests specific device permissions to operate reliably.

3.1 Exact Alarm & Scheduling

Exact alarm permission allows the application to schedule alarms at the times configured by users.

This permission helps ensure alarms trigger accurately, including during supported Doze and low-power conditions.

3.2 Camera Permission

Camera permission is required exclusively when users configure or use an Object Scan challenge.

The camera is used to capture and verify objects as part of the challenge.

3.3 Foreground Service & Audio

Foreground service functionality supports ongoing alarm operation.

It helps ensure that alarm sounds and vibration continue while an alarm is active, until the user dismisses the alarm or completes the required challenge.

3.4 Notifications

Notification permission allows Alarm Sun to:

  • Display alarm notifications.
  • Show ongoing alarm status.
  • Display relevant alarm alerts.
  • Inform users about scheduled alarm activity.

Users can manage notification permissions through their device settings.

3.5 Vibration

The application may use the device vibration capability to provide alarm alerts and wake-up notifications.

4. How We Use Information

Information handled by Alarm Sun is used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing alarms.
  • Scheduling alarm notifications.
  • Maintaining user-selected alarm configurations.
  • Running object scan challenges.
  • Managing alarm sounds and vibration.
  • Maintaining application preferences.
  • Improving application reliability and functionality.

We do not use locally stored alarm information for advertising or sell personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage & Security

Alarm Sun stores its alarm configurations and application preferences in device-local storage, including:

  • SQLite database.
  • Android SharedPreferences.

These storage mechanisms allow the application to retain alarm settings and preferences on the user’s device.

We take reasonable measures to protect locally stored application information.

However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Alarm Sun does not sell users’ personal information.

Alarm configurations and preferences stored locally are not intentionally shared with third parties.

The application does not require users to connect social media accounts or share contacts.

If third-party services, analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services are introduced, their data practices will be disclosed in an updated Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Since alarm configurations and preferences are stored in your device’s local storage (SQLite / SharedPreferences), you retain control over your locally stored application data.

You can delete your application data by:

  • Clearing Alarm Sun’s application data through Android settings.
  • Uninstalling Alarm Sun from your device.

Clearing application data or uninstalling the application removes locally stored alarms, settings, and logs associated with the application, subject to Android backup or restoration behavior.

Users should be aware that deleting the application may result in the loss of all configured alarms.

8. Children’s Privacy

Alarm Sun is a general-purpose utility application.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application’s core functionality.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that requires action under applicable privacy laws, we will take appropriate steps.

Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of mobile applications.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Understanding what information is processed.
  • Requesting information about data handling.
  • Requesting deletion of applicable personal information.
  • Managing camera and notification permissions.
  • Restricting device permissions through Android settings.

Since most alarm configurations are stored locally, users can manage or delete this information directly from their devices.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect:

  • New application features.
  • Changes in device permissions.
  • Improvements to data storage practices.
  • Changes in applicable privacy requirements.

When updates are made, the revised policy will be published with an updated effective date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.
